# Prompt: Visible hand + one-time “Pinch here”

## Goal
Make onboarding obvious and finishable with a **swipe slider** (or one tap).

1. **See my hand** — While the camera is on, always show a live hand skeleton.
2. **Slide to continue** — Final step is a slider: move your hand left → right across it (like unlocking). Tap still works. Pinch is optional backup.

## UX requirements
- Visible hand skeleton on camera preview + full-screen overlay.
- Tutorial: open palm → move → **swipe slider**.
- Slider thumb follows the hand; fill completes setup at ~78%.
- Horizontal swipe gesture also completes the step.

## Acceptance
- Swiping across the slider finishes onboarding without a precise pinch.
- Tap on the slider also completes setup.
